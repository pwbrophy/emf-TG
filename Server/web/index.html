How to play

  1. Tap Join Game and enter your name.
  2. When there’s room, you’ll be let into the lobby. Choose a tank: its team is shown on the card.
  3. When the host starts the game, turn your phone sideways. You’ll see through your tank’s camera.
  4. Left stick drives. Slider turns the turret. FIRE shoots, then needs a moment to reload.
  5. After the game you can play again: you’ll join the back of the queue.
