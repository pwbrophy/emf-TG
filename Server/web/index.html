How to play

  1. Tap Join Game and enter your name.
  2. When there’s room, you’ll be let into the lobby. Choose a tank: its team is shown on the card. On a 2-player tank one of you drives and the other is the gunner.
  3. When the host starts the game, turn your phone sideways. You’ll see through your tank’s camera.
  4. Left stick drives. Slider turns the turret. FIRE shoots, then needs a moment to reload.
  5. Hits from behind do the most damage. When you’re destroyed, drive back to your base tag to respawn.
  6. Drive over a capture point’s tag to take it: points you hold score for your team.
  7. After the game you can play again: you’ll join the back of the queue.
