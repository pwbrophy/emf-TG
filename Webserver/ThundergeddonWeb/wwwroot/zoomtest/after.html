This page copies the game's zoom settings so we can test them on an iPhone. Please open it in Safari (not a preview inside Messages, Mail or Files). The green box in the top-left corner tracks zoom. Please screenshot it when you're done.

  1. First, check that page zoom in the box shows 100%. If it doesn't, tap aA and set it back to 100%.
  2. Double-tap this text, the title, the buttons and the list below, quickly and several times.
  3. Tap Go to game screen, then turn the phone sideways. Double-tap and mash FIRE, the video area, the HP bar and the timer. Hold the joystick with one thumb while you tap FIRE with the other. Try pinching.
  4. If the page zooms in, try to zoom back out with a pinch or a double-tap. Does it work?
