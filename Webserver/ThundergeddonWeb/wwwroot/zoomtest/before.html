This page copies the game's zoom settings so we can test them on an iPhone. Please open it in Safari (not a preview inside Messages, Mail or Files). The green box in the top-left corner tracks zoom. Please screenshot it when you're done.

  1. First, check that page zoom in the box shows 100%. If it doesn't, tap aA and set it back to 100%.
  2. Tap Go to game screen. Play it like the game for a couple of minutes. Hold the joystick with your left thumb while you mash FIRE and the turret pill with your right. Double-tap FIRE, the video, the HP bar and the text labels. Try pinching. Turn the phone between upright and sideways a few times as you go.
  3. Watch the green box. The zoom and game text lines should stay at 1.00x. If anything turns red, or the screen grows or overlaps, screenshot it straight away. Then try to get back to normal with a pinch or a double-tap, and screenshot again.
  4. Optional: tap Back and double-tap around the lobby too.
